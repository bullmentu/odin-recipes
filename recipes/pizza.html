Description

Mini pizza is a delicious and easy-to-make dish that is perfect for lunch, dinner or a snack. It is made using a ready-made pizza base topped with tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, pepperoni and fresh vegetables. The pizza is seasoned with oregano and olive oil to give it extra flavour. It is then baked in the oven until the cheese is melted and the crust is golden and crispy. This recipe is simple to prepare, requires only 8 ingredients and is perfect for beginners.

Ingredients

  • 2 ready-made pizza bases
  • 4 tablespoons tomato pizza sauce
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella cheese
  • ½ green bell pepper
  • ½ red onion
  • 6 slices of pepperoni
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil

How to prepare

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over each base.
  4. Sprinkle grated mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Add chopped peppers, onion and pepperoni.
  6. Sprinkle oregano and a little olive oil over the pizzas.
  7. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the cheese melts and the edges become golden.
  8. Remove from the oven, allow to cool slightly and serve.
