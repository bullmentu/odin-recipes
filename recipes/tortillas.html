Tortillas Recipe

Home

Description

This homemade tortilla recipe is simple, quick, and requires only a few basic ingredients. They are soft, flexible, and taste even better when served warm!

Ingredients

  • Cooked chicken (grilled or shredded)
  • Lettuce
  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Red onion
  • Sweetcorn
  • Grated cheese
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper

How to prepare

  1. Prepare the chicken
  2. Cook the chicken
  3. Prepare the salad
  4. Make the sauce
  5. Fill the tortilla
  6. Fold the tortilla
  7. Serve and enjoy!
